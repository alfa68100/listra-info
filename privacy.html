Privacy Policy

Listra · Last updated: 7 October 2026

Listra is a private tool used by a single person for his own Etsy shop (see the home page). This page explains what data it handles.

Whose data

Listra connects only to the Etsy and Pinterest accounts of its owner. It does not collect data from customers, buyers or any other Etsy users, and it has no accounts, sign-ups or tracking of visitors.

What it accesses on Etsy

With the owner's permission (Etsy OAuth), Listra can read the owner's shop and listing information and create and update draft listings in the owner's shop. It does not publish listings, and it does not read buyer messages or orders.

What it accesses on Pinterest

With the owner's permission (Pinterest OAuth), Listra can read the list of the owner's boards and create Pins on those boards. Each Pin contains the image, title and link of one of the owner's own Etsy listings. A Pin is created only after the owner confirms it in Listra. Listra does not read other people's content or messages.

Where data is stored

Listing data and the Etsy connection are kept on the owner's own computer. Listra does not send them to a server operated by the owner or by any third party for storage.

Third-party services

  • Etsy: receives the draft listings the owner sends.
  • Pinterest: receives the Pins the owner confirms.
  • Google Gemini API: supplier text and product photos may be sent to Google to help write or check listing text and to prepare images. Google's own terms apply to that processing.

Sharing and selling

No data is sold or shared with advertisers.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Listra at any time in the Etsy and Pinterest account settings (connected apps), which revokes its access.

Changes and contact
