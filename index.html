Listra

A private listing tool for one Etsy shop.

Listra is a small software tool built and used by one person, the owner of the Etsy shop NameAuraStudio, to prepare product listings for that shop. It runs on the owner's own computer.

What it does

  • Reads product details from a supplier page that the owner opens.
  • Helps write the listing text, tags and prices, and checks them against the supplier's information.
  • Sends the result to the owner's own Etsy shop as a draft. The owner reviews and publishes it by hand. Listra never publishes listings by itself.
  • Prepares Pins (image, title and link of one of the owner's own Etsy listings) for the owner's own Pinterest boards. A Pin is posted only after the owner confirms it.

Who can use it

Listra is not a public service. Only the owner uses it, and only with the owner's own Etsy and Pinterest accounts.
